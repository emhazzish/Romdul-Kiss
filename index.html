About Romdul Kiss

after the national flower of Cambodia, Romdul Kiss brings the authentic flavors of the Khmer Empire to your table. We use traditional "Kroeung" spice pastes and fresh, locally sourced ingredients to ensure every bite is a tribute to our rich heritage. Enjoy the flavors of wonders in Cambodia through our Cambodian Cuisine. This Cusisine serves not only traditional food made with local ingredients but also the, sweat and tears of our personal chef. Using Special sauces and spices into crafting the most delcious and memorable taste for our dear lovley customers.

Our Mission

"At Romdul Kiss, our mission is to be the living fragrance of Cambodian hospitality. Just as the Romdul flower graces the Kingdom with its timeless scent, we strive to enchant our guests through the authentic artistry of Khmer cuisine. We are dedicated to preserving ancestral recipes, sourcing the finest local ingredients, and serving every dish with the grace and warmth of the Cambodian spirit—bringing the heart of our heritage to your table, one 'kiss' of flavor at a time."

Our Service
